OUR PURPOSE

Three systems. One connected you.

Vital Three Initiative is a youth-led health education community focused on the relationships between mental well-being, skin health, and cardiovascular health.

We share accessible learning resources, encourage curiosity, and bring people together through educational sessions. Our goal is to help everyone ask better questions about their health.

VITAL THREE

Knowledge creates connection.

When we understand how our bodies work together, we can make more informed choices for our well-being and support our communities.

EXPLORE THE PILLARS

The science of being connected

Each pillar matters on its own — and each influences the others.

Mind

Understand stress, emotional well-being, sleep, and how the nervous system influences the body.

Skin

Explore skin health, the skin barrier, and the relationship between stress and skin conditions.

Heart

Learn about circulation, cardiovascular well-being, movement, and everyday heart-healthy habits.
